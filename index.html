CX Internal Reporting App

The CX Internal Reporting App is a private, internal software integration operated by CX Enterprises LLC. It retrieves accounting data belonging to CX Enterprises LLC and its affiliated companies, with the authorization of each company's administrator, for internal bookkeeping, consolidated reporting, and archival purposes.

This application is not distributed to the public, is not listed in any application marketplace, and provides no products or services to third parties.
